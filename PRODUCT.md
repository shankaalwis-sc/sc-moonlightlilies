# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: static HTML, CSS and a little vanilla JS, no build step. The user asked for a brand-new standalone folder and repo, separate from the Secret Santa app, and named no framework; a static page hosts anywhere (subdomain, CDN, Shopify-adjacent hosting) and has nothing to maintain.

## Users

Spa Ceylon customers in Sri Lanka (the linked store is lk.spaceylon.com, priced in Rs) choosing festive-season gifts for the people who matter most to them. They arrive from campaign channels (social, email, in-store QR) and decide whether the Moonlight Lilies range is where they will buy this year's gifts.

## Product Purpose

A campaign landing page for Spa Ceylon's Festive 2026 gift collection, **Moonlight Lilies**. It presents the collection, tells the visitor enough of the campaign story to make the gifts feel meaningful, and sends them on to two destinations:

- Explore the range: https://lk.spaceylon.com/pages/festive-2026 (the shop, primary action)
- Read The Legend of the Moonlight Lilies: https://moonlight.spaceylon.com/ (an illustrated 32-page flipbook, secondary action)

Success is a visitor clicking through to the range, or to the Legend and then the range.

## Positioning

The collection is built on Spa Ceylon's own illustrated legend: lilies on a sacred lake in the Kingdom of Ceylon that bloom only when a great blessing draws near. The Queen forbids anyone to take them, and instead asks the island's painters, weavers, jewellers, potters and perfumers to carry the lilies' beauty into things that can be given. The gifts are that idea made real: beauty shared, not taken.

## Operating Context

- Campaign tagline (on the key visual): "A curated collection of gifts for those who mean the most."
- Store page title: "Moonlight Lilies Festive 2026 Gifting Collection". The Legend closes on "This festive season, the legend comes alive once more."
- Visitors are often on phones.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Real range as listed on the festive-2026 store page (October 2026):

- Moonlight Lilies Beauty & Fragrance Collection (described by the store as limited-edition)
- Moonlight Lilies Bath & Body Collection
- Four lilies, each as a Pampering Set (bath and shower gel, body lotion, perfume, soap, hand cream) and as Island Treats (creme perfume, luxury soap, body lotion, glossy lip butter): Moonlight Lily, Twilight Lily, Starlight Lily, Magical Lily
- Luxury Soap Collection (3 x 50g, 3 x 100g)
- Intensive Hand Cream Collection (4 x 10g, 3 x 30g)
- Creme Perfume Collection (3 x 8g floral fragrances)
- Island Cracker Trio (3 x 20ml floral body sprays)

Prices change on the store, so the landing page does not show them. The store owns cart and checkout.

## Brand Commitments

- Spa Ceylon, "Ayurveda Wellness". Gold logo mark.
- Brand type: Trajan for headers, menus and labels; Goudy (Goudos) for body. Free stand-ins: Cinzel and Sorts Mill Goudy.
- The landing page uses the key visual's fonts and colours (user instruction).
- Key visual: night palace, lotus lanterns on water, burgundy theatre curtains, a seated queen, the gift range in lotus-print packaging.
- User direction (22 October 2026): the page is a single full-screen hero: the key visual, Explore the range, Read the Legend and one line of copy. No header, no footer, no sections below it, no product listings. Phones load a dedicated 1080 x 540 mobile key visual.

## Product Principles

1. The story serves the gift: every part of the legend shown should make the range feel more meaningful, then hand the visitor to the shop.
2. Shopping is one click from anywhere on the page.
3. Real products, real names, no invented claims, no prices that can go stale.
4. It should feel like the key visual: the same night, the same lamplight, the same lettering.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. The key visual's words are baked into the image, so the page must expose them as text. Motion respects reduced-motion preferences.
